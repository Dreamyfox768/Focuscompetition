# 🌸 Focus Buddy

Your little buddy for better focus! ⏱️


✨ Features

- ▶️ Start, stop, and reset your timer
  
- ⏱️ Track your focus time
  
- 🏆 Keep an eye on your personal best
  
- 💙 A clean, calming design to keep you focused


🌷 How to Use
- Click Start to begin your focus session.
- Click Stop when you need a break.
- Click Reset to start fresh.
- Beat your personal best and celebrate! 🎉

💖This is for brains that struggle to focus because world is full of cool things.💖
